## Overview

![Representative works from the Math+Code+Art tutorials, exhibitions, and research](/book/representative-image.jpg)

Mathematics, code, and art are often taught as separate disciplines, yet they share a
common language of pattern, structure, and transformation. Math + Code + Art bridges
these fields through hands-on Jupyter notebook tutorials, student art exhibitions, and
research, inviting readers to discover how mathematical ideas become creative tools. At
its core is a simple belief: computational thinking expands artistic practice, and
creating art builds intuition for mathematics and computation.

The book is organized around three complementary pillars:

- **Education**: Open-access Jupyter notebook tutorials, each centered on a
  mathematical or computational concept and culminating in a visual or auditory
  creation.
- **Creation**: Student art exhibitions that demonstrate how these ideas evolve into
  original artistic works through genuine creative exploration.
- **Exploration**: Research that pushes the same mathematical concepts toward new
  frontiers in science, engineering, and creative computation.

Each chapter includes links to the corresponding source code and Jupyter notebooks in
the [Math + Code + Art GitHub repository](https://github.com/jiayinlu19960224/Math-Code-Art),
enabling readers to experiment with, modify, and build upon every project presented in
the book, along with references to related exhibitions and research papers for those
who wish to explore the ideas further.

We hope this book serves as an open educational resource for students, educators,
artists, and anyone curious about the intersection of mathematics, code, and art.
